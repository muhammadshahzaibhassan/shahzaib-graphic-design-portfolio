## About the series

Over ten days I followed the light through small mountain villages, waking before sunrise and walking until the last colour left the sky.

![Village rooftops lit by the first morning sun](../../assets/projects/travel-photo-story/image-1.jpg)

## Editing approach

I kept the edits natural: gentle contrast, warm highlights and careful colour grading in Lightroom so the places look the way they felt.

![Portrait of a local shopkeeper in warm evening light](../../assets/projects/travel-photo-story/image-2.jpg)
